How ZedMarket should work

The plan for turning this demo into a real marketplace
🛒Discover and compare

Customers search local listings and explore international supplier offers.

🏪Seller storefronts

Approved businesses create profiles, upload products and manage enquiries.

🌍International sourcing

Buyers request quotations and compare minimum order quantities, shipping and landed costs.

🛡️Trust and verification

Business identity, supplier documents, product claims and reviews need checks before trust badges are shown.

📦Orders and delivery

Later versions can add order tracking and approved delivery partners.

💳Safe payments

Payments should only be added through compliant providers after adult-led legal and security setup.

Prototype safety note: Sample products, prices and supplier categories are fictional examples, not verified offers. The basket works only in this browser session and does not place orders or collect payments. Supplier enquiry buttons show a draft message only. Do not send money or personal information based on demo listings.
